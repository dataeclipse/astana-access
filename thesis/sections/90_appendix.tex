\chapter{Test Results}
\label{app:tests}

The test suite is run with \texttt{pytest}. All 108 tests pass in a few seconds. Table~\ref{tab:tests} lists what each group verifies.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Unit tests}
\label{tab:tests}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{4.4cm}r>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X}
\toprule
Test file & Tests & What is verified \\
\midrule
\texttt{test\_names.py} & 22 & Normalisation of school names, extraction of numbers and types, compatibility of school kinds \\
\texttt{test\_addresses.py} & 26 & Street name variants in Kazakh and Russian, lanes kept apart from streets, house numbers, address splitting, district hints, alias resolution through former street names, refusal to guess an unknown house \\
\texttt{test\_stations.py} & 9 & Parsing of house lists and street walks from the decision, inclusion and exclusion clauses, rivers as boundaries, polygon construction, preservation of official totals by the allocation \\
\texttt{test\_access.py} & 8 & Distance decay functions, allocation of each child once, exclusion of children beyond the allocation radius, zero access without a facility, Gini extremes and weights, exact Theil decomposition \\
\texttt{test\_optimization.py} & 5 & Plot sizes of the planning standard, statutory radii, detection of free plots, the maximal covering solution, the capacity constraint \\
\texttt{test\_listing.py} & 6 & School numbers read from listings, projection of listed positions, preservation of station totals when floors are replaced, parsing of the official age table and agreement of its totals with the model, year labels of official tables \\
\texttt{test\_catchments.py} & 5 & Parsing of official service territories: house lists, initials not taken for street types, ranges, whole streets, settlements, removal of notes on language of instruction; weighted median of walking distance \\
\texttt{test\_plan.py} & 5 & Agreement of the transcribed construction plan with the official totals by year, placement of planned schools by number and by listed point, refusal to place an unresolved site, sums of reach by site and by district, places read from the detailed plans, greedy ranking of sites for the atlas \\
\texttt{test\_register.py} & 5 & Reading of the official list of schools: school numbers, districts, years of commissioning and registration, linking of Binom schools by name, count of pupils beyond two shifts; preschool capacity and attendance filled for every organisation \\
\texttt{test\_uncertainty.py} & 9 & Agreement of the fast index of the Monte Carlo simulation with the reference E2SFCA, allocation without noise equal to the population model, official district totals kept under noise, points without a district ignored, percentile bands, cohort ageing and change ratios of the projection to 2028, the two-year step of the population projection, bounded change ratios, districts adding up to the city \\
\texttt{test\_siting\_models.py} & 8 & Land shares around candidate cells, labelling of the cell nearest each site, log transform of count features, sums over H3 rings without the cell itself, clipped area shares of cells, metrics of the housing forecast, weighting and capacity caps of the two-objective ranking \\
\midrule
Total & 108 & All passed \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Besides unit tests, each stage was checked against an observation that it did not use: positions against official coordinates, population against official district totals, reconstructed territories against the districts named in the decision, the residential classifier against house lists and the 2GIS listing, programme schools and imputed floors against the same listing, and the floating catchment load against school enrolment. These checks are reported in Chapters~\ref{ch:methods} and~\ref{ch:results}.

The range in brackets runs from the lowest to the highest of the other variants: no migration, the change of 2024--2026, and for districts a share of the city held at 2026.
