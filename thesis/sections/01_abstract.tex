\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

The work develops an open, data-driven framework for assessing spatial accessibility to social infrastructure and evaluating its siting, applied to state schools and preschools in Astana. Population below the district level, which is not published, is reconstructed from official district totals, the territories of 386 polling stations and classified building footprints, and misses district populations by 10 per cent against 26 per cent for WorldPop; children are counted with the official age structure of each district. Walking accessibility is measured with a floating catchment index based on the official design capacity of schools, whose load step is checked against their official enrolment and service territories. Nura, the fastest-growing district, is the worst served for schools and preschools, but over nine-tenths of inequality lies within districts; the statutory radius is met on foot for 22 per cent of school-age children. Official enrolment shows 1.7 pupils per design place, most in Saraishyk. The sites of the national Comfortable School programme were chosen where land was free and reached 84 per cent of the coverage attainable within 1 km on land that was free in 2023; the plan for 2026--2028 reaches 15 per cent of the children without a school within 1 km, while the 17 best of the 238 school sites already reserved in the city's detailed plans would reach 2.4 times as many.
