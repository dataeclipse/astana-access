Method & data

How the atlas is built and checked

Every figure comes from one reproducible pipeline run on open data and official replies. Each model is checked against an observation it did not use.

Population model against official district totals

Predicted over official population of each district on 1 January 2026. Mean absolute error: polling-station model 10%, WorldPop 26%, floor area alone 28%.

Sources

    Limits

    • Population below the district level is an estimate; the official age split of Almaty and Saraishyk is replaced by their combined share, which school enrolment supports.
    • Site screening counts children without a state school within walking distance and ignores capacity; for the plan’s schools capacity never binds.
    • Detailed-plan sites are building contours on the geoportal; their reservation status is not published.
    • Preschool capacity and attendance come from register cards compiled on one day; the waiting list is published for the city only.
    • The full ranking of the 238 detailed-plan sites, with district, coordinates and children reached at 1 and 1.5 km, is kept in the project repository as reports/pdp_site_ranking.csv.
